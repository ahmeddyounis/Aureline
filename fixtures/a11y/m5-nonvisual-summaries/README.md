# M5 Non-Visual Summary Fixtures

These fixtures are valid, export-safe non-visual summary catalogs that exercise the
auto-narrowing behavior the canonical support export keeps green. Each one keeps a
summary row for every governed surface kind, the shared and summary vocabulary sets
intact, every provisional presentation state exercised, and the conformance-review,
consumer-projection, and release-posture invariants satisfied — the difference is which
surface narrows and why. They are minted from the same seed builder as the canonical
export by `aureline_shell_m5_nonvisual_summaries`.

## proof_stale_narrowed.json

The chart summary's assistive-tech proof has gone stale. The surface narrows from
Stable to Beta and keeps its `proof_stale` downgrade trigger, so the gap is disclosed
while the chart keeps its quantified structure, drill-down routes, chart-description
text alternative, metadata view, and object identity intact. Demonstrates that stale
proof narrows the claim rather than hiding the surface.

## bridge_unavailable_narrowed.json

The artifact viewer's OS accessibility bridge is unavailable. The surface narrows from
Stable to Preview, drops its `non_visual_fidelity` to `degraded_accessible`, and keeps
its `bridge_unavailable` downgrade trigger. Its image alt text and metadata view stay
present on a reopenable banner-detail fallback. Demonstrates that when the bridge is
gone the artifact still exposes its non-visual alternative, rather than the meaning
being dropped behind pixels.
