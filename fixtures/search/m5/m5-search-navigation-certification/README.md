# M5 search/navigation certification fixtures

These fixtures sit **above** the M5 search/navigation qualification matrix. The
qualification index freezes *what* each search surface must prove; this
certification index proves that proof is **current**, lane by lane, and narrows
the claim automatically the moment it is not.

- `packet.json`: canonical certification index. All four search/docs/graph depth
  lanes — query-session identity, ranking explainability, saved-query privacy,
  and navigation continuity — are `certified` on `fresh` evidence. Each lane
  cites its own evidence packet, schema, doc, artifact, fixture corpus, and
  record kind, and every claim surface (product, CLI/headless, docs/help,
  support export) is in parity.
- `retest_pending_stale_evidence.json`: degraded packet where the
  navigation-continuity lane's evidence is older than the recheck window, so the
  lane fails closed to `retest_pending`; the other lanes stay `certified`.
- `limited_degraded_source.json`: degraded packet where the ranking-explainability
  lane's source packet is itself degraded and the support-export surface drops
  parity (still projecting a `certified` claim the lane no longer proves), so the
  lane fails closed to `limited` and the overclaiming surface is held back.
- `unsupported_missing_evidence.json`: degraded packet where the
  saved-query-privacy lane's evidence is missing, so the lane fails closed to
  `unsupported`; its broad claim cannot be sustained until the evidence is
  restored.

Each fixture is regenerated by
`crates/aureline-search/examples/dump_m5_search_navigation_certification_packet.rs`
and replayed against the seeded packet by the inline tests in
`crates/aureline-search/src/m5_search_navigation_certification/tests.rs`.
