# M5 supportability qualification fixtures

- `packet.json`: canonical qualification index for every claimed supportability
  surface on every claimed M5 profile, binding each surface to its own Support
  Center, environment-explainability, precedence, consent, crash-intake, or
  handoff lane proof plus the supportability drills (diagnosis latency,
  consent-sheet accuracy, export-mode parity, crash-loop recovery, exact-build
  intake) that back it. Every row is `qualified` and covers the desktop and
  CLI/headless deployment modes.
- `consent_drill_stale_narrowed.json`: degraded packet where the
  consent-sheet-accuracy drill is stale, so every send-capable surface
  (support-bundle consent and supportability handoff) narrows to
  `local_self_diagnosis_only`; local-save stays first-class while the
  team-share and formal-support send paths are held unverified.
- `environment_evidence_stale_blocked.json`: degraded packet where the
  environment-explainability evidence is stale, so that surface blocks as
  `blocked_unverified` and the Support Center that binds environment status
  narrows to `limited_profile_scoped`; the precedence, consent, crash-intake,
  and handoff surfaces keep their own fresh proof and stay green.

Each fixture is regenerated by
`crates/aureline-support/examples/dump_m5_supportability_qualification_packet.rs`
and replayed against the seeded packet by the inline tests in
`crates/aureline-support/src/m5_supportability_qualification/tests.rs`.
