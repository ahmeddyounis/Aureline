# Certify language-provider arbitration, diagnostic convergence, and refactor preview/rollback truth on every claimed framework and structured-artifact lane

Stable contract for the certification that gates whether a claimed
framework-pack or structured-artifact lane keeps a certified grade. Where
the matrix freezes which posture a lane *may* claim, this certification
records whether the lane has *earned* that claim through checked-in
evidence drills.

This document is the human-readable companion to the certification truth
packet. The canonical record is checked in at
`artifacts/language/m5/provider_refactor_certification_truth_packet.json`
and validated by the boundary schema at
`schemas/language/provider_refactor_certification_truth.schema.json`. The
certification is owned by `aureline-language`
(`crates/aureline-language/src/provider_refactor_certification_truth_packet/`)
and regenerated by the
`dump_provider_refactor_certification_truth_packet` example.

## Why this exists

The matrix, the typed refactor transaction, the wide-scope refactor
fallback, the diagnostic-cluster, and the semantic-result arbitration
packets each freeze one slice of language-provider truth. A lane can pass
all of them on paper and still be untrustworthy if the behaviour was
never exercised against real evidence. This certification is the
exit-gate: it refuses to keep a lane at a certified grade unless provider
arbitration, diagnostic convergence, refactor preview completeness, and
deterministic rollback are each *proven* by a checked-in drill, and it
narrows the lane automatically the moment that evidence is missing,
stale, or downgraded — rather than letting the lane inherit the broader
framework-pack marketing language.

The certification extends, and never weakens, the launch-language
refactor transaction safety model: a lane that certifies a mutating
refactor carries the same typed preview, completeness labeling, and
rollback discipline the matrix and refactor-transaction packets already
pin. This packet only adds the proof verdicts and evidence drills on top.

## What the packet asserts

The packet covers the five claimed artifact-family lanes —
`framework_pack_lane`, `notebook_cell_lane`, `generated_source_lane`,
`structured_artifact_lane`, and `code_understanding_graph_lane`. Each
lane carries a `lane_certification` headline row that names its acting
provider family and a concrete certification verdict, plus one
certification row per dimension:

- **Certification verdict** (`lane_certification`) — `certified`,
  `provisionally_certified`, `narrowed_below_certified`,
  `blocked_pending_evidence`, or `withdrawn`. A lane that claims
  `certified` support while its verdict says `blocked_pending_evidence`
  or `withdrawn` is refused (`verdict_support_mismatch`).
- **Provider arbitration** (`provider_arbitration_certification`) — binds
  one `arbitration_proof_class` (single-provider, agreement-and-disagreement
  proven, loser preserved, downgrade-honesty proven, or crash/quarantine
  recovery proven) and the `conflict_class`. Whenever the conflict is a
  disagreement, the losing provider and downgrade reason MUST stay
  inspectable; collapsing it to a ranking-only result is refused
  (`disagreement_collapsed_to_ranking_only`).
- **Diagnostic convergence** (`diagnostic_convergence_certification`) —
  binds one `convergence_proof_class` (single-source, multi-source
  converged-and-labeled, per-source provenance preserved, suppression
  state preserved, or freshness labeled).
- **Refactor preview** (`refactor_preview_certification`) — co-binds the
  `refactor_transaction_class` and a typed `completeness_class`. A
  mutating refactor that labels its preview `unsupported` (or leaves it
  unbound) is refused (`mutation_bypasses_preview_or_rollback`).
- **Rollback determinism** (`rollback_determinism_certification`) —
  co-binds the `rollback_path_class` and a `rollback_determinism_class`
  (deterministic rollback proven, checkpoint replay verified,
  regeneration replay verified, manual-review-only, or
  nondeterministic/unsafe). A certified lane that binds a
  nondeterministic/unsafe rollback is refused
  (`rollback_determinism_not_proven`).
- **Generated-artifact policy**
  (`generated_artifact_policy_certification`) — binds one
  `generated_artifact_policy_class` so generated assets carry an explicit
  policy rather than being edited as if hand-authored.
- **Evidence drills** (`evidence_drill_admission`) — each row binds one
  proven drill. The packet requires the full set across all lanes before
  any lane keeps a certified grade: `fixture_repo_drill`,
  `notebook_case_drill`, `generated_case_drill`, `config_case_drill`,
  `partial_scope_drill`, `provider_crash_quarantine_drill`, and
  `rollback_determinism_drill`. A missing required drill is refused
  (`missing_required_evidence_drill`).

Every row also binds a `support_class`, an `evidence_class`, a
`known_limit_class`, a `downgrade_automation_class`, and a
`confidence_class`, carries at least one evidence ref, and excludes raw
source bodies, secrets, and ambient authority. A row that claims
`certified` while any binding is unbound is refused
(`certified_with_unbound_binding`); a row narrowed below `certified`, or
declaring a non-`none_declared` known limit, or binding a non-`none`
downgrade automation, MUST carry a disclosure ref.

## Automatic narrowing

The `downgrade_automation_class` on each row records how the lane narrows
without manual intervention: `auto_narrow_on_missing_fixture`,
`auto_narrow_on_provider_unavailable`,
`auto_narrow_on_conflict_unresolved`, `auto_narrow_on_preview_partial`,
`auto_narrow_on_stale_provenance`, `auto_demote_on_low_confidence`,
`auto_block_on_missing_evidence`, or `manual_only_pending_review`. Any
row failing arbitration, convergence, preview, or rollback truth, or
whose evidence is missing or stale, drops the packet's promotion state to
`narrowed_below_stable` or `blocks_stable` instead of inheriting an
adjacent certified row.

## Consumer surfaces

`framework_pack_panel`, `structured_artifact_runner`, `preview_surface`,
`compatibility_report`, `archetype_scorecard`,
`release_narrowing_automation`, `support_export`, `help_about`,
`service_health`, and `conformance_dashboard` all read this packet
verbatim and re-export it through the support-export wrapper without
admitting private material. A projection that collapses any closed
vocabulary, or fails to preserve the packet id, is refused.

## Crosswalk

This certification consumes the leaf vocabularies frozen by
`provider_refactor_matrix_truth.schema.json` (provider family, conflict,
completeness, rollback path, refactor transaction, generated-artifact
policy, evidence, known-limit, downgrade-automation, and confidence
classes) rather than minting a local synonym set, so a lane's
certification verdict reads in the same terms as the matrix posture it
certifies.
